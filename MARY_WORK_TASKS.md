## CREATIVE IDEAS TO EXPLORE

**For @dreamlifevisuals (Trending/Funny/Silly):**
- Trending audio + unexpected transition (your face, silly reaction, object reveal)
- "POV: You're [relatable situation]" format with on-screen text
- Duet/stitch concept (react to trending, add your take)
- Quick lip-sync to trending audio with exaggerated facial expression
- "Trying [random trend] for the first time" — be goofy, be authentic
- Comment bait: "This is why [relatable frustration]"

**For @tyusesai (Static Photos, Growth Narrative):**
- Before/after comparison (if you have photos) with hook like "30 days difference"
- "What I learned on Day X" — static photo + text overlay + voiceover
- Carousel format: Photo 1 (problem), Photo 2 (attempt), Photo 3 (solution), Photo 4 (result)
- "A week in: here's what's working" — use static photos to show progress
- Mistake angle: "I did this wrong on Day X, here's how to avoid it"
- "This one trick changed everything" — static photo with bold on-screen text

**For @useclaudeai (Learning Series):**
- "Day X: I tried Claude for [specific task]" — screen recording + voiceover + text
- Vulnerability angle: "Day X: I failed at this" — be honest about what didn't work
- "Comparing Claude to ChatGPT on [task]" — side-by-side, on-screen text
- Mistake angle: "Day X: I used Claude wrong, here's what I learned"
- Fear angle: "Day X: My coworker asked why I use Claude..." (story hook)
- Tip angle: "Day X: Here's a hack nobody knows about"
- Mix in static photos of you + text overlay, not just screen recordings

**General Creative Rules:**
- Be weird. Be vulnerable. Be real. Generic content performs worst.
- Watch comments on similar posts. What are people asking? Answer that.
- Try something that feels slightly risky or unconventional. That's what converts.
- If you think "that's too silly," it's probably the right move.
- Copy what works, but add YOUR personality. Don't be a robot remix.

## NOTES

- **Don't stress perfect.** Reps 1-20 are learning. By rep 50 you'll see patterns.
- **One thing per day.** If today's focus is "tighten hooks," focus there. Don't also try to optimize captions.
- **Document everything.** Your rep log is your progress tracker. Look back at week 1 vs week 4. You'll see the jump.
- **Study winners daily.** 30 minutes a day studying someone else's video teaches you more than editing 10 mediocre ones.
- **Ask specific questions.** When you're unsure, ask Tyler: "Is this hook weak or is it the pacing?" Not just "thoughts?"
- **Be creative.** This list is a starting point. If you have an idea for content, run with it. Weird > Perfect.
