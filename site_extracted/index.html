My Filter

How I Pick Products

Every product I post clears these three bars. No exceptions.

01
Would I buy it myself?
If I wouldn't spend my own money on it, I'm not asking you to. I care about my health and appearance — this is the lens everything gets filtered through.
02
Does it actually work?
I test everything before I post it. Results matter. I'm not here to hype garbage and burn your trust. If it doesn't deliver, it doesn't make the cut.
03
Is the value real?
You can find junk anywhere. I'm looking for products where the price-to-result ratio makes it an obvious yes. If it's not a clear win, I won't waste your time.
