The method
How a video becomes a sale.

Every post is a decision. Hook, angle, timing. Nothing random. This is the system.

01
The product is the last thing Tyler thinks about.
Starting with the problem. What are they lying awake about? What would make them stop scrolling and actually feel something? The video is just the delivery mechanism.
02
His word is the product. He protects it.
Deals he doesn't believe in get turned down. When Tyler says something works, his audience listens. That trust is the inventory.
03
One second. That's the whole game.
Lead with pain, not product. The second it feels like an ad, people are gone. Tyler writes hooks that feel like he read their mind. Because he did the work to get there.
04
TikTok Shop, not just TikTok.
GMV, commissions, how the algorithm decides what to push, when to get on a product early. Tyler watches what's gaining traction in Shop before picking a product. By the time we talk, he already has angles.
05
When something works, he moves that day.
Skit, talking head, demo, voiceover. Every format gets tested. When one lands, variations run the same day. Same angle, different delivery, before the algorithm moves on. That's how you stack wins.
