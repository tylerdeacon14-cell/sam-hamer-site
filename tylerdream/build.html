What is a media kit?
A media kit is your creator resume. It's a single link you send to brands that shows who you are, what you create, how big your audience is, and what it costs to work with you. Instead of copy-pasting your stats into a DM, you send one clean link and look like a professional.
Why do you need one?
Brands receive hundreds of creator pitches. The ones without a link get ignored. A media kit tells a brand everything they need to decide in under 60 seconds: your niche, your numbers, your content, your rates. Without it, you're asking them to do work you should have done for them.
