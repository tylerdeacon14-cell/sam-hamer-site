# Editing Style Guide - Danny Methodology

Last Updated: May 3, 2026

## Core Philosophy

80% problem / 20% life change. Lead with the PROBLEM. As soon as it looks like an ad, they're gone. Trash the category—position against what they've tried and failed. Short content converts better. Delay dopamine—withhold the reveal.

**Golden Rule:** Never open with product. Never open positive.

---

## Hook Strategy (First 1-2 Seconds)

Stop the scroll before they scroll. 5 components working together:

### 1. On-Screen Text
- 5 words max (use oneoften.com outlier score to verify viral potential)
- Black/white bubbles only - no fancy fonts, no animations
- Basic sans-serif (Arial, Helvetica)
- High contrast, high motion
- Context + curiosity gap (tell them what they'll miss if they scroll)

Examples that work:
- "Here's what nobody gonna tell you on TikTok"
- "I got caught using AI — best thing that ever happened"
- "Nobody at my job knows I use AI"

### 2. Visual Hook
- Background: Office with warm yellow/orange light converts well. Tesla/car background converts well. Test outside, different rooms. Background is part of the visual hook.
- Movement: Clapping, putting glasses on/off, shaking something, dipping grapes in sour cream
- Contrast: High motion, weird/unexpected
- Rage bait visual hooks get comments (which signals engagement to algo)

### 3. Verbal Hook
- Direct callout to target demographic (who are you speaking to? Call them out immediately)
- Relatable phrase most people think but won't say out loud
- Bold, ballsy, honest
- Fewest words possible, active voice

### 4. Super Hook
- One extra sentence after the hook - builds curiosity or credibility
- "I couldn't believe what happened next"
- "My wife snuck into Justin Bieber set last night"
- Creates FOMO - tell them what they'll miss if they scroll

### 5. Format
- Talking head = 80% of what works. Master this first.
- Then explore: Skit, Green Screen, Faceless, Text-Only
- When a format wins → replicate same script in all other formats immediately

## Video Structure (60-90 Seconds)

### Hook (First 5-10 seconds)
Use all 5 hook components above. Stop the scroll in 1-2 seconds.

### Problems (70% of video)
- Don't mention product
- Don't show product
- Don't hint at product
- Tell a story around the pain
- Make them feel SEEN
- If you think you're talking too much about problems, you're not

Show, don't tell. Problems all the way through. Stack objections and pain points.

### Bridge (~70% mark)
- Explain the product only if needed (don't over-explain)
- Add credibility: studies, reviews, "I found a user who said..."
- Keep it brief
- This is where you earn the reveal

### Life Change (Transformation)
- Not features — transformation
- First-person experience only
- "I haven't had a migraine in a week. My eyes feel brand new."
- Show what life looks like NOW

### CTA / Why Buy Now (Last 15-20 seconds)
- Scarcity: "These sold out last week"
- Urgency: "Sale is ending"
- Price comparison: "Same price as a cheeseburger at McDonald's" (NEVER say actual price—TikTok AI flags it)
- Buy 2 for discount: "Add two to cart, one's basically free"

### Objection Handling (Woven throughout)
- "I can get this cheaper on Amazon" → address it
- "This probably won't work for me" → "I was skeptical too. I've tried everything."
- More objections = higher conversion rate

## Content Funnels

### Top of Funnel (TOF)
- New product, cold audience, broad problem angles
- Cast wide net
- Lots of variation to find what sticks

### Middle of Funnel (MOF) — BEST CONVERSIONS
- Counterposition angles — attack alternatives they've tried
- Target 70% of content here once product has traction
- This is where winners live

### Bottom of Funnel (BOF) — AVOID
- Trains TikTok to show content to buyers only
- Kills reach
- Don't use unless you're already past initial scale

## Video Formats (Ranked by Conversion)

1. **Skit** — Highest converting
   - Don't reveal product until end
   - Problems all the way through
   - Feels organic, not an ad

2. **Talking Head** — Classic, on camera
   - Simplest to film
   - Most reliable
   - Master this first

3. **Green Screen** — React/overlay
   - Can be strong if hook is tight
   - Don't let it distract from problem

4. **Faceless** — Product demo + voiceover
   - Works for certain angles
   - Less personal, lower conversion typically

5. **Text-Only** — Stretch it out, delay dopamine
   - Use when hook is exceptionally strong
   - Delay the reveal to maximum

## Copy & Language Rules

- Write like audience is 12 years old (simple words, no jargon)
- No buzzwords: never say "amazing," "incredible," "game-changer"
- Problem first, solution second
- Active voice, fewest words possible
- No claim language — first-person experience only
- "Supports digestion" not "cures digestion"
- "Designed to support" not "will fix"
- Personal testimonials must not imply product directly caused result

---

## Pacing & Retention

### Early Retention (First 3 seconds)
- Hook is everything
- If drop-off is under 3-5 seconds → hook is the problem

### Watch Time
- Keep it SHORT (under 90 seconds ideal)
- Every second counts
- Cut dead air
- Cut anything that doesn't move story forward

### Completion Rate
- If low completion → solution/CTA not landing hard enough
- Tighten the ending
- Make the reveal more satisfying
- Add urgency

---

## Music & Audio

- Use trending sounds (but not ONLY trending)
- Match beat to pacing
- Audio should enhance, not distract
- Silence can be powerful (underused)
- Voice (if voiceover): clear, energetic, relatable

---

## Transitions

- Keep them minimal
- Jump cuts work
- Match to beat if music-driven
- Don't use transitions to hide edits—own the edits

---

## Captions

- Support on-screen text, don't repeat it
- Use for emphasis on key moments
- Keep them readable (no tiny fonts)
- Color: white with black outline for readability
- Timing: sync to voiceover or beat

---

## Color Grading

- Warm tones tend to convert better
- Consistency within video
- Don't oversaturate
- Match lighting to mood (problem = darker, solution = lighter/warmer)

---

## Product Reveals

- Earned, not given
- Come late in video (70% mark or later)
- Problem must earn the reveal
- Once revealed, show transformation immediately

---

## GMV MAX & Momentum

- TikTok algo picks up converting videos and amplifies with ad budget
- Be early — under 200 creators = cheaper amplification
- When GMV MAX picks up a video → film reply videos immediately to eat up ad budget
- Look for upward 90-day trend = GMV MAX in motion

---

## Daily Metrics to Check

- Follow-to-view ratio: target ~1%
- GMV per video
- CTR on product link
- Which format is winning
- Watch time vs completion

---

## Common Mistakes

- Opening with positive energy (wrong—open with problem)
- Mentioning product too early (wrong—delay reveal)
- Spending too much time on solution (wrong—80% problem)
- Using fancy fonts on-screen text (wrong—simple only)
- Talking TO audience instead of speaking TO their problem
- Giving up at 30 reps (wrong—volume negates luck, 500 reps to elite)

---

## Key Principles

1. **One product at a time** — 15-20 videos before moving on
2. **3 videos/day per account** — Move with speed
3. **Check metrics daily** — Never blame the algorithm
4. **Negative beats positive** — Lead with problem always
5. **Delay dopamine** — Withhold the reveal
6. **Strategy is commoditized** — Iteration distance off rep 1 is the differentiator
7. **Volume negates luck** — Consistency > perfection

Study winners → explode → hold strong bricks → improve weak → restack

- Topic
- Angle
- Hook
- Story structure
- Visual format
- Key visuals
- Audio

When you find a winner, break it down into these 7 bricks. Remake what worked. ~500 reps to elite; most quit at rep 10-30.

## Reference: Callaway Hook Formula

3 aligned components for viral on-screen text:

- **Visual** — High motion/contrast
- **Text** — Context + curiosity gap
- **Spoken** — Fewest words, active voice

Direct contrast > implied. +100 to -100 = 200 arc (full spectrum of emotion/interest in the hook alone).

## Reference: Callaway Volume Method

Study own winners first. Remake what worked. No winners? Study top creator's best video → break into 7 bricks → remake.

~500 reps to elite. Most people quit at 10-30 reps. This is the game.

## Editing Checklist Before Export

- [ ] Hook stops scroll in 1-2 seconds
- [ ] Problems take up 70% of video
- [ ] Product reveal is earned
- [ ] No mention of product in first 70%
- [ ] On-screen text is 5 words max, high contrast
- [ ] CTA is clear and urgent
- [ ] Watch time is optimized (no dead air)
- [ ] Completion rate will be high (ending is satisfying)
- [ ] Format matches the script
- [ ] Audio enhances, doesn't distract
- [ ] First-person experience only (no claims)
- [ ] Objections are woven in
- [ ] Pacing matches music/beat
